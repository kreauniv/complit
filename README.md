# CompLIT: Programming Literacy

This course introduces programming to students who might want to do a fair bit
of programming but might not have the background or prior exposure to get going
with it. We also expect it to offer some new perspectives to students who
consider themselves adept at it, but might've fallen prey to poor habits formed
early.

The course is based on [How to Design Programs][htdp] but works between the
lines of the material introduced there and tries to be even gentler in some
places while being a little more aggressive in other areas.
